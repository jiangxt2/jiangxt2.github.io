## Topic path

- Tasks: splitting independent computation into schedulable remote work.
- Actors: managing stateful distributed objects and their lifecycle.
- Scheduling: how resource declarations, placement constraints, and dependencies affect execution.
- Observability: using logs, state, and metrics to investigate distributed failures.

Future notes will separate Ray Core from higher-level training, data, and serving capabilities.
